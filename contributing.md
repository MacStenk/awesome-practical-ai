# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to add something.

## Guidelines

- Entry format: `- [Name](url) - Short, objective description. Ends with a period.`
- No marketing language, no "the best", no exclamation marks.
- The item must still work. Dead links, archived repos, or discontinued channels get removed, not added.
- Add your entry at the bottom of the relevant category.
- One pull request per entry, so it's easy to discuss and merge separately.
- Say in the pull request why it belongs here, and what you actually did with it.

## What belongs here

Things a practical builder - not a full-time programmer, not a total beginner - can actually use. If it takes a CS degree to get value out of it, it's probably too advanced for this list.
